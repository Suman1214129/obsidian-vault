*Discrete mathematics is <mark>the study of separate, distinct mathematical objects</mark>. Unlike calculus (which uses flowing, continuous numbers like 1.5 or 3.99), discrete math deals with distinct values like integers (1, 2, 3), letters, or steps. Think of it as the foundational math for computers, logic, and puzzles.*
