# SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM

## The hardest part of learning is finding the right person to learn from.

Today, finding a tutor still looks like:

**Search → Shortlist → Ask Around → Guess → Try → Switch**

There is no reliable way to answer the most important question:

> “Who is the right tutor for ME?”

### For learners:

- Finding the right fit is difficult
    
- Quality is hard to judge beforehand
    
- Personalization depends on the individual tutor
    
- Switching tutors means starting the search again
    

### For tutors:

- Student acquisition is inconsistent
    
- Growth depends heavily on referrals
    
- Their expertise is difficult to discover
    

> The world has millions of teachers.  
> The problem is finding the right one.
